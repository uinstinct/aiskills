## Codebase Patterns
- Workspace Cargo.toml at root includes `src/external`; `cargo build` runs from root and outputs to `target/` at root level (not `src/external/target/`)
- Release workflow uses separate build jobs that upload artifacts, then a final `release` job downloads and publishes
